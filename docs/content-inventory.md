# Content Reference — Skills & Bosses

A reference of the healing spells and boss encounters currently playable in the game.

## Player Skills

| Skill | Mana | Cast Time | Cooldown | Target | Unlocked By | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Greater Heal | 5 | 2s | - | Ally | Starting skill | Mana-efficient heal for 40 health. |
| Renew | 5 | Instant | 4s | Ally | Starting skill | Heal-over-time: 10 health every 3s for 15s (50 total). |
| Circle of Healing | 8 | Instant | 7s | Up to 5 allies | Beat Boss 1 (Normal) | Heals up to 5 allies for 40 health each. |
| Power Word: Shield | 5 | Instant | 4s | Ally | Beat Boss 2 (Normal) | Shields the target for 15s, absorbing up to 40 damage. |
| Shadow Word: Death | 4 | 1.5s | - | Enemy | Beat Boss 3 (Normal) | Deals 12 damage. |

You start with Greater Heal and Renew, and unlock the rest by beating bosses. Only 5 spells can be
active on your action bar at a time — you choose which ones, and which slot and hotkey each one
gets, between fights.

## Bosses

Each boss can be fought on Normal or Heroic difficulty. Beating Normal unlocks Heroic for that boss.

### Boss 1

| Difficulty | Ability | Cast/Channel | Cooldown | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | Auto Attack | 2.5s cast | - | 20 damage, favors tanks |
| Normal | Toss Boulder | 1s cast | 5s | 30 damage, favors healers/DPS |
| Normal | Enrage | 3s cast | 25s | Self-buff: +40% haste & cooldown reduction for 12s |
| Heroic | Auto Attack | 2s cast | - | 25 damage |
| Heroic | Toss Boulder | 1s cast | 4s | 35 damage, favors healers/DPS |
| Heroic | Enrage | 3s cast | 15s | Self-buff: +40% haste & cooldown reduction for 12s |

### Boss 2

| Difficulty | Ability | Cast/Channel | Cooldown | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | Auto Attack | 1.5s cast | - | 10 damage to 2 targets |
| Normal | AoE | 1.5s channel (3 ticks) | 10s | Hits up to 5 players, 12 damage per tick |
| Heroic | Auto Attack | 1.5s cast | - | 12 damage to 2 targets |
| Heroic | AoE | 1.5s channel (3 ticks) | 10s | Hits up to 5 players, 12 damage per tick |
| Heroic | Throw Rock | 1s cast | 12s | 30 damage, single target |

Boss 2 is the fight without a self-buff ability — its challenge comes purely from sustained damage.

### Boss 3

Fought with a 10-person group (2 Tank / 2 Heal / 6 DPS).

| Difficulty | Ability | Cast/Channel | Cooldown | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | Auto Attack | 2.5s cast | - | 20 damage, favors tanks |
| Normal | Necrotic Curse | 1s cast | 8s | Curse dealing 100 damage over 4s, no upfront hit |
| Heroic | Auto Attack | 2.5s cast | - | 25 damage, favors tanks |
| Heroic | Necrotic Curse | 1s cast | 6s | Curse dealing 130 damage over 4s, no upfront hit |

Both difficulties give the boss 500 max health.
